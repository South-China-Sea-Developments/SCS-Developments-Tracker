Pertamina and Marathon signed a production-sharing contract on 30 November 1979 for oil exploration in Natuna Bloc B (24,700 sq km, partly in the area disputed with Vietnam), for a reported $8.5 million signature bonus; Vietnam had protested the tender and the awards of Blocs A and B, and Indonesia did not respond to the protest.

Actors: Pertamina; Marathon Petroleum (contractor)

Indonesia

1979-12-06 (day) Natuna Islands area, sub-region 10 — an area (Natuna Islands), sub-region only suggested: declarative ev-26d27c646d

Foreign Minister Mochtar Kusumaatmadja told the parliament's Commission on Defence and Foreign Affairs on 6 December 1979 that Vietnam's reported protest over Indonesia's award of an oil exploration contract in the disputed Natuna area was merely a formality putting Vietnam's position on record, and that it did not mean Indonesia should halt oil exploration around the Natuna Islands.

Actors: Foreign Minister Mochtar Kusumaatmadja; DPR Commission I

Indonesia

1979-12-11 (day) Natuna Blocs C, D1 and D2, South China Sea, sub-region 10 — an area (Natuna Islands), sub-region only suggested: demonstrative ev-a4530c96dd

Pertamina signed production-sharing contracts on 11 December 1979 with Mobil Oil for Natuna Blocs D1 and D2 and with Amoseas for Natuna Bloc C (partly in the area disputed with Vietnam), and Minister of Mines and Energy Subroto said at the signing that Indonesian oil exploration in the South China Sea would continue on schedule despite the absence of an agreed continental-shelf boundary with Vietnam.

Actors: Pertamina; Minister of Mines and Energy Subroto; Mobil Oil and Amoseas (contractors)
