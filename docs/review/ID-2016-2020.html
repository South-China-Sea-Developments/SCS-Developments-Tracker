2020-05-26 (day) South China Sea / North Natuna Sea, sub-region 10 — an area (Natuna Islands), sub-region only suggested: declarative ev-dd7720f962

On 26 May 2020 Indonesia's Permanent Mission to the UN sent a note verbale to Secretary-General António Guterres, in the exchange triggered by Malaysia's December 2019 CLCS submission, stating that China's nine-dash line map and claim of historic rights lacked any basis in international law, invoking the 12 July 2016 arbitral award, and affirming that Indonesia has no territorial dispute with China.

Indonesia

2020-06-12 (day) South China Sea / North Natuna Sea, sub-region 10 — an area (Natuna Islands), sub-region only suggested: declarative ev-fbc674a7d4

On 12 June 2020 Indonesia sent a second note to the UN Secretary-General replying to China's 2 June letter, which had proposed negotiating 'overlapping claims': Indonesia stated that under UNCLOS 1982 there were no overlapping claims with China and that it saw no legal reasoning under international law to negotiate maritime boundaries with China; Foreign Minister Retno Marsudi said there was no reason to negotiate.

Indonesia

2020-07-26 (day) North Natuna Sea off Natuna Regency, Riau Islands, sub-region 10 — an area (Natuna Islands), sub-region only suggested: coercive ev-57d6723d07

On Sunday 26 July 2020 the Bakamla patrol boat KN Pulau Dana-323 chased a Vietnamese-flagged fishing boat suspected of illegal fishing in the North Natuna Sea, which discarded its net, burned tyres to obscure visibility and attempted to ram the patrol boat; after a warning shot the patrol boat fired on the vessel until it slowed and was captured with about two tons of fish aboard (one of several Vietnamese boats seized in the North Natuna Sea that year).

Actors: Maritime Security Agency (Bakamla), KN Pulau Dana-323 (Lt. Col. Hananto Widhi)

Source

the foreign vessel had been detected on Sunday by Indonesian patrol boat KN Pulau Dana-323

Suggested coding and reasoning

Type coercive · domains physical · issues fisheries · dyads IV

Firing on and seizing a Vietnamese fishing boat in the overlapping EEZ area is physical interference imposing punishment; coercive. Recorded as the year's representative instance of continuing arrests of Vietnamese boats. [confidence: medium]

Indonesia

2020-09-11 – 2020-09-14 (day) North Natuna Sea (Indonesian EEZ), sub-region 10 — an area (Natuna Islands), sub-region only suggested: coercive ev-63d7872263

From the night of 11 September 2020 Bakamla tracked China Coast Guard ship 5204 inside Indonesia's EEZ in the North Natuna Sea; on 12 September the patrol ship KN Nipah 321 closed to within a kilometre, and the two ships exchanged radio messages each affirming its nation's claim to the area, Bakamla shadowing the vessel until it left the North Natuna Sea at 11:20 on Monday 14 September after almost three days.

Actors: Maritime Security Agency (Bakamla), KN Nipah 321
