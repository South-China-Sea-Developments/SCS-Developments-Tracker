# 25: Frequency sources and candidate tallies

**What to build:** Two aids for the *frequency* criterion, which needs two comparable figures for the same
kind of act in consecutive years (or a source stating the increase) and which Chubb satisfied from counting
series, never from his own tally of the news. (1) `docs/notes/frequency-sources.md`: an index, per claimant
and practice, of the series that count (what they count, who publishes, years), with a generated section
listing every Chubb move worded as an increase and the sources he cited for it. (2) `python -m pipeline.tally`
→ `docs/reports/candidate-tallies.md`: the harvested candidates per claimant, year and practice (practice
read off the summary by keyword), labelled as a pointer to where a frequency source is worth looking for,
never a count to cite. Asked for on 2026-11-20, the second day of review, so a frequency decision takes
minutes rather than twenty.

**Blocked by:** 19

**Rules.** The tally's practices are keyword rules in `PRACTICES` order, first match wins; a candidate can
sit in the wrong column and the report says so. The index's entries marked *(verify)* are from the
maintainers' knowledge of the field, not yet checked in this project: a coder confirms a series' coverage
before citing it, and adds the series he used. The generated section is refreshed in place by
`python -m pipeline.tally --chubb-increases`.

**Not done:** the report is regenerated by hand (not by CI or the nightly task); no series is fetched or
verified automatically; weekly-run candidates appear in the tally only through `data/events.csv`.

**Status:** built 2026-11-20.
