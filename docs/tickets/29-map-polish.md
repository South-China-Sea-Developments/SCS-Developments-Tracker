# 29 — Map polish after launch

Maintainer, 2026-11-22, after the home map went live.

- **The zoom hint** ("use ctrl + scroll to zoom the map") covered the map each time the page scrolled
  over it. On a desktop it is now hidden (the + and − buttons and ctrl + scroll still zoom); on a phone
  it is a small pill at the foot of the map, with no veil.
- **More precise features, the far view unchanged:** from zoom 5.5 in, Natural Earth's reef outlines and
  minor islands (public domain, `web/static/geo/`), and every named feature of the gazetteer as a faint
  grey point, with its name from zoom 7.6. Both maps (home and Events).
- **Banner:** seven options mocked up for the maintainer (a separate preview); his choice comes in a
  later change.
