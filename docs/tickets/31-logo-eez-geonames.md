# 31 — Official logo, EEZ boundaries, more place names

Asked by the maintainer on 2026-11-23 ("the other 2 artifacts to refine the map"), built the same day.

- **Logo:** the official Asia Society Policy Institute logo, supplied at high resolution, in white on the
  menu band of every page (330×113 file, shown at 32 px high, 28 px on phones), with a thin divider before
  the site's name.
- **EEZ boundaries:** the indicative maritime-zone layer is replaced by the Marine Regions Maritime
  Boundaries Geodatabase v12 (Flanders Marine Institute, CC BY 4.0, doi:10.14284/632): treaty, median,
  unsettled and 200-nautical-mile lines that involve a claimant, clipped to the sea and simplified, as a
  map layer only (not redistributed as data). The switch reads "EEZ boundaries (where settled or claimed)".
  Where claims overlap (most of the Spratlys and Paracels) Marine Regions draws areas, not lines, so few
  lines cross the middle of the sea; that is the source's choice, stated rather than filled in.
- **More place names:** a GeoNames layer (CC BY 4.0) adds 37 reefs, shoals, banks, islands and rocks inside
  the ten sub-regions and more names (pinyin romanisations, French, Vietnamese, Filipino, English variants)
  for 52 known ones; 321 features in all. A name another feature already carries is never added. It placed
  one more existing move (144 of 388): most unplaced moves name no feature at all. The extra names matter
  for future harvested sources. Generated by `pipeline.gazetteer_geonames`; the project's own corrections
  still win.
- Credits on the methodology page: GeoNames, Marine Regions, Natural Earth.
