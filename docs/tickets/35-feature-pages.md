# 35 — Feature pages

Improvement 3 of the eight agreed on 2026-11-23.

- A page per reef, shoal or island that a published move is placed at or a claimant holds (81 now),
  at `features/<english-name>.html`: its English name, its names in Chinese, Vietnamese, Filipino and
  Malay and the other spellings the gazetteer knows, its sub-region and position (with the Wikidata
  link), who holds it (ticket 36), and every move placed there, oldest first, each linking to its case.
- A places index, `features.html` ("By place"), grouped by sub-region with the number of moves at each
  and a ring in the holder's colour; linked from the Cases page.
- Linked from the home map (a feature's detail panel: "All moves at … since 1970"), from the case pages'
  insets ("Named: …") and back to the map ("See it on the map").
- The map's address now names a feature by its page's slug, so features whose gazetteer id is Chinese
  can be linked too.
