# 12: New claimants: 1970–2015 backfill

**What to build:** Malaysia, Indonesia and Taiwan have a 1970–2015 move series coded with Chubb's rules from archival sources, reviewed through the normal queue. Worked in chunks (per claimant, per decade); this ticket tracks progress.

**Blocked by:** 11

**Status:** prepared; coding not started (2026-10-19, PR #21). Two gates before coding starts: decision 07's 2026-10-19 rule (No project-coded entry is merged into the dataset until the maintainer has reviewed it himself on its review page; no further candidate sets are compiled or queued until he asks for them.) and decision 03's sequencing (the first CN/VN/PH 2016+ review proves the routine first). So this ticket delivers the source lists, the procedure and the progress table, and no candidates.
