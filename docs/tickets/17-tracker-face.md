The maintainer chose (2026-10-27): the home page is an app-style page — a dark sidebar (title, search, filters with coloured dots, the events in view listed newest first) beside a full-height dark map — with the charts on their own Trends page; the ASPI "South China Sea Research Initiative" banner he supplied (logo and title as sent) is the site header on every page; the colours come from that banner (the logo's navy, the sea's teals); no dark mode; the charts prettier.
