# 39 — Cite, download or embed a view

Improvement 7 of the eight agreed on 2026-11-23.

- Under "Copy a link to this view" on the home map, "Cite, download or embed this view" opens:
  - a citation of the view: the window, claimant, sub-region or feature shown, the dataset version, the
    view's link and the date accessed, with Chubb's MATS credited;
  - "Download the map as an image": a PNG of the map as drawn (basemap, shading, counts), with a title,
    the view, the dataset and the basemap's attribution;
  - embed code: an iframe of the same view with `?embed=1`, which shows the map and its caption alone,
    with a link back to the full view, and no opening curtain.
