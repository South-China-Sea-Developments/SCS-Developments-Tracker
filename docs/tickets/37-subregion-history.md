# 37 — A sub-region's score over time

Improvement 5 of the eight agreed on 2026-11-23.

- Selecting a sub-region on the home map now shows, in its panel, a line of its score at every window
  end (or, in build-up mode, every year), on the map's fixed scale and with the current filters and
  weighting; the current window is marked. Hovering reads out a window's score; a click moves the map
  to that window.
- Windows after the last coded year are drawn dashed and grey, marked "being coded", so the empty
  recent years do not read as a fall in assertiveness.
