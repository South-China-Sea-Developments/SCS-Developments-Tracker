# Wikidata gazetteer

`data/reference/gazetteer.csv` is generated by `python -m pipeline.gazetteer_wikidata` from Wikidata. **Licence: CC0 1.0** (all Wikidata structured data), so the rows are redistributed here; each carries its item's QID and the retrieval date (first retrieval 2026-10-15).

How it is made:

1. One SPARQL query to `https://query.wikidata.org/sparql` for every item with coordinates (P625) inside the box 106.5–120.0°E, 2.5–21.5°N (the box that encloses the ten MAVD polygons).
2. Point-in-polygon against `data/reference/mavd_subregions.geojson`: only items inside one of the ten sub-regions are kept (439 on first run).
3. A second query for those items' labels in English, Chinese (zh and zh-tw), Vietnamese, Filipino, Malay and Indonesian, their aliases in those languages, and their instance-of (P31).
4. Items are kept when their type is a maritime feature (reef, island, shoal, atoll, ocean bank, cay, rock, islet, low-tide elevation, guyot, artificial island, disputed territory, island group, archipelago …) or, for untyped items, when the name reads as one; ships, wrecks, eclipses, battles, airports, lighthouses, villages, boundaries and the like are dropped (283 kept).

Wikidata's coordinates for small reefs are approximate (typically the feature's centre to a few hundred metres, occasionally a few kilometres); a test checks every feature lies in the sub-region it states. Names and aliases are whatever Wikidata editors recorded: some are ordinary words (the Filipino alias "Timog" for South Reef, "Kota" for Loaita), which is why `pipeline.gazetteer` only matches names that contain a feature word or have at least two words, and why `gazetteer_extra.csv` can remove an alias (`-Name`).

`data/reference/gazetteer_extra.csv` is the project's own: additions (Union Banks, Pratas, Natuna) and corrections (pinyin and Filipino aliases, "Reed Bank" as the English name of Q7306628, the Spratly Islands and the South China Sea Islands as a whole spanning several sub-regions). Edit that file, never the generated one.
