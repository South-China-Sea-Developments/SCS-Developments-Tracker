Key points for this project, taken from the appendix:

- A case is an **intensification relative to the previous calendar year** by qualitative novelty, increased frequency, or expanded geographic scope; not every assertive action is a case.
- Collection is two-stage: a wide **master timeline** of all plausibly relevant developments, then a **refined series** of cases coded with the four-type typology.
- Each case is given a likely **duration**; absence of evidence of equivalent actions the next year is usually treated as **diminishment**, with exceptions for long gaps between data points.
- Stricter evidentiary standards were applied to recent events than to distant ones, to counter present-centric bias in the information supply.
- Only non-cooperative behaviour is coded; conciliatory actions are deliberately excluded.
- Inter-coder agreement on the 132 PRC cases: 92.4 % and 87.1 %.
