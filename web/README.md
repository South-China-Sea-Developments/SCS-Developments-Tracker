# Public site

A self-contained static bundle built from `data/*.csv` by `python -m web.build`. No framework, no build chain: Jinja2 templates, one stylesheet, three scripts, one image (the programme banner), two vendored fonts (Outfit and Instrument Sans, both OFL). Relative paths only, so the output folder can be dropped onto any host (GitHub Pages for now; ASPI later) or opened from disk.

## Pages

- `index.html` the tracker (`map.html` renders the same page so older links keep working): a sidebar with the title, the count of events in view, a search box, the filters (claimant with its dot as the legend, type, and under "More filters" domain, issue and counterpart), the time controls, and the events in view newest first — beside the event map (MapLibre GL, vendored; events, polygons and the basemap style inline, the events also written to `data/events.json`; OpenFreeMap's vector tiles, fonts and sprites are the only external requests — free, no key, no stated limits; the bundled style `web/static/vendor/openfreemap/dark-en.json` is the Positron style recoloured dark by `web/tools/derive_dark_style.py`, with English labels; the tile URL inside it is the one line to change for another tile host); `trends.html` (moves by year stacked by claimant or type, one small multiple per claimant with moves, the four types); `issues-domains.html` (issue and domain mentions by year, by claimant and by sub-region); `subregions.html` (choropleth by period with a claimant selector, moves by sub-region and type, one chart per sub-region over time); `cases.html` searchable case browser (client-side filtering of an inline index); `cases/<move_id>.html` one page per move with coding, events, sources and an inset map of the sub-regions (simplified polygons inlined, ~1 KB); `methodology.html` (codebook summary, provenance, how to cite, and the machine–human agreement report, also written to `data/agreement.json`); `downloads.html` (the files at the latest release tag, with checksums, served from `downloads/` inside the bundle, plus citations in text, BibTeX and CFF). The build publishes accepted moves only: a pending move has no page, no row and no count anywhere on the site (ticket 17).

## Design notes

Subject: a research dataset about contested waters. Audience: researchers, analysts, journalists. Job: trends at a glance, then down to the evidence.

- **Tokens (tickets 15–17):** the block at the top of `style.css` holds the brand: display face and weights, body face, the kicker style, paper and surfaces, the banner's colours (`--navy` from the logo, `--sea`/`--sea-deep`/`--sea-mid`/`--sea-light`/`--teal`/`--lagoon` from the water), the band and its rules, ink, accent, focus, the map's polygon and sea colours, radius, the sidebar width, measure and spacing. The programme banner the maintainer supplied (`static/img/banner.png`, logo and title as sent) is the header of every page, so the site presents itself as the institute's (decision 06, amended 2026-10-27). One colour scheme: no `prefers-color-scheme` rules. `map.js` reads the polygon colours from the tokens; `derive_dark_style.py` states the basemap's colours as literals. The data palettes below the block do not follow the brand.
- **Identity device:** the banner, cropped from the top on wide screens so the logo, the title and its rule stay in view, on a band of the sea's own colour; under it the navy navigation band with small letter-spaced capitals. The tracker beneath is in the manner of CFR's Global Conflict Tracker: a dark sidebar (title, count, search, filter groups with the claimant dots as the legend, time, the list) beside a dark map that fills the viewport; the rest of the site is white paper, hairlines, data and type. Lists are rows divided by hairlines, not cards; figures are cards with a soft shadow because they are the one thing on the page that is a picture.
- **Home page:** the tracker fills the viewport under the header (`height: max(100vh − header, 640px)`; `map.js` measures the header); the sidebar scrolls on its own; at phone width the map comes first, viewport-high, then the sidebar. The events in view are listed newest first with date, claimant, a clamped line of summary and a link to the move; the search box looks through summary, place, claimant, move id and sub-region names. Nothing on the page describes the project's progress.
- **Type:** Outfit (a light geometric sans, close to the banner's title face) for titles, h2, the sidebar's title and count, dates in the lists and move descriptions — weight 300 on the dark planes where it is large, 600 as headlines; Instrument Sans for interface, labels, charts and body. The one all-caps style is the kicker (small, bold, letter-spaced, in the manner of the wordmark): the navigation, the kicker over page titles, the sidebar's group labels, the brand name and the footer headings.
- **Colour:** page plane white, ink blue-black, links and kickers in the sea's teal (7:1 on white), focus rings in a darker amber on paper (4:1) and the dashboard's amber on the dark planes; the navigation band the logo's navy, the sidebar and the map the banner's sea. Claimants use the dataviz categorical palette in fixed slot order (China, Vietnam, Philippines, Malaysia, Indonesia, Taiwan) so a filter never repaints survivors; a claimant with no moves in the data has no chip and no panel on the Trends page. Move types are an ordered intensity scale and use one blue ramp, pale (declarative) to dark (use of force). Issues and domains use the categorical slots in fixed order on their own page. The choropleth uses the sequential blue ramp. All palettes validated with the dataviz validator.
- **Marks:** stacked columns ≤ 24px wide, 4px rounded data-end, surface-coloured gaps between segments (2px, 1px when bands are narrow), hairline solid gridlines, muted axis ticks, tabular figures on ticks and in tables.
- **Pills in the case list:** a dashed pill marks a move pending the author's confirmation; a solid pill marks project coding (any move not from MATS 1.02), so the two provenances never pass for one another.
- **Case browser filters:** within a group (type, domain, issue) any checked value matches; across groups all must match; the claimant chips are a show/hide set like the overview's, so unchecking all shows nothing.
- **Interaction:** one hover tooltip per column (values lead, labels follow, line keys), keyboard focus shows the same; each figure's filters sit directly above it (on the home page the map's claimant chips and the charts' are independent sets); every chart has a table-view twin (the WCAG-clean equivalent, and the relief for the light-mode hues under 3:1 contrast: Philippines, Malaysia, Indonesia and the declarative step). Keyboard users read the table; the column hit targets are pointer-only so a chart is not 46 tab stops.
- **Event map:** events are the move's colour (claimant); a filled disc has its own coordinates, a hollow one stands at a named feature; events sharing a position stack into one disc carrying their count; unplaced events are a neutral count pill on their sub-region; the sub-region polygons are pale dashed outlines on the dark basemap. The basemap is OpenFreeMap's Positron recoloured dark (slate land, navy sea, light labels with dark halos, English-only labels: a Chinese, Thai or Vietnamese label would be a second script competing with the data) so the data stays the loudest thing on the map. Time is one month slider plus a window selector rather than a two-handle range: one control, and "the year ending here" is how the dataset thinks.
- **Responsive:** columns shrink to a 5px band before the chart scrolls (46 years fit a phone); if it must scroll it opens at the latest years with a fade on the left edge. Small multiples go to one column below 440px.

Tried and rejected: three-column small multiples on desktop (bands too thin to read); hover targets only on years with data (a year with none should still answer); an 8px minimum band on phones (hid 2007–2015 behind an unsignposted scroll); focusable column hit targets (184 tab stops per page).
