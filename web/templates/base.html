SCS Developments Tracker

A continuation of Andrew Chubb's Maritime Assertiveness Time Series to the present and to six claimants, coded with the same rules. Machines propose candidate events; every move in the dataset was decided by a person.

Provenance

Data for 1970–{{ meta.mats_last_year }}: Chubb, MATS 1.02, used with permission. Later years and additional claimants: project coding. Dataset version {{ meta.dataset_version }}; build {{ meta.version }}, {{ meta.build_date }}.
