How this page is worked out

Every pair, in numbers

Moves by the row’s claimant toward the column’s in the years chosen: the count, the mix by type (declarative to force, light to dark), and the share coded coercive or force. Grey: moves aimed at all claimants, counted but not in the share. Under {{ 10 }} moves no share is given.
