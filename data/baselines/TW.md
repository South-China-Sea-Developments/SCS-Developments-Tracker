# Taiwan (Republic of China): baseline actions before 1970

**Scope note.** The claimant is the Republic of China (ROC) government as a continuous actor: its 1945–1949 actions were taken from the mainland (labelled "ROC, mainland period") and its post-1949 actions from Taiwan; both count toward the 1970 baseline from which MATS "moves" are measured. "Action" means a physical, administrative, legal or diplomatic step by ROC state organs (navy, Ministry of the Interior, Executive Yuan, MOFA) asserting or exercising jurisdiction over the Pratas (Dongsha/Tungsha), Paracels (Xisha/Shisha), Macclesfield (Zhongsha/Chungsha) or Spratlys (Nansha). The 1950 withdrawal is included as a de-escalation marker, not an assertive move, because the 1956 return cannot be read without it. Every entry below carries a URL that was actually opened and a verbatim quote (≤ 25 words); items that could not be verified are listed separately. Four pages were blocked by the fetch tool and are noted under "Unverified leads".

## Unverified leads

- **1958 PRC territorial-sea declaration.** No source opened in this session records any ROC statement or position on the PRC's 4 Sep 1958 declaration. MOFA-2016 has no 1958 entry; Ma Ying-jeou's 2016 speech mentions only the 1958 UN Conference on the Law of the Sea. Treat as absent unless archival work (Academia Historica/MOFA archives) shows otherwise.
- **Date of the 1950 withdrawal.** The Australian Parliamentary Library chronology (citing Hayton 2014, pp. 63–64) dates the abandonment of Woody Island and Itu Aba to "October 1949"; Hayton-AMTI, Hayton 2024 and Eiterjord (citing Till 2009) say May 1950; the ROC Presidential Office says Itu Aba "starting in June 1950". The May/June 1950 dating (post-fall of Hainan) is preferred here.
- **"12 December 1945" Nansha Administrative Office.** The CGA-Hist translation (PCA Annex 910) lists "On 12 December 1945, the Government of the Republic of China established the Nansha Administrative Office under the jurisdiction of the Guangdong Government". This is almost certainly a typo for 12 Dec 1946 (the same document gives the arrival as 12 Dec 1946); not entered separately.
- **Destruction of Cloma's markers/structures (1956).** APH chronology paraphrases Hayton 2014 (pp. 66–67) as ROC vessels having "dismantled structures he had built"; Doyo (Inquirer) writes Cloma's men "found some markers destroyed by Taiwanese troops." Plausible but neither is a primary or scholarly text opened verbatim here.
- **1950 Philippine phosphate "theft".** CGA-Hist: "In 1950, people from the Philippines landed on Taiping Island and other islands, wantonly stealing from the phosphorous mines." Indicates ROC awareness, not an ROC action; no corroboration found.
- **Number of features renamed in 1947.** MOFA-2016 says "the MOI announced the names of 97 islands, cays, and reef flats"; NMDIS says 172 (incl. 12 archipelagos). Chris P.C. Chung (2016, *Modern China*) was not accessible for adjudication.
- **Protests to South Vietnam over the Paracels** (1956 Pattle Island occupation; 1961 Saigon decree attaching the Paracels to Quang Nam) — not found in any source opened; MOFA-2016 records only Spratly-related protests (Aug 1956, 1963).
- **1946 Pratas ship/commander and exact date** — CGA-Dongsha gives only "summer 1946"; Eiterjord gives 1946.
