# Indonesia: baseline actions before 1970

**Scope note.** Indonesia claims no Spratly (or Paracel) feature; its South China Sea stake is sovereignty over the Natuna group (then administered inside Kabupaten Kepulauan Riau, Riau Province) and jurisdiction over the waters and continental shelf around it, which China's nine-dash line later overlapped. The pre-1970 baseline therefore consists of unilateral maritime-jurisdiction assertions (the 1957 Djuanda Declaration, the 1960 archipelagic-baselines law whose northernmost basepoints sit on the Natuna islands, the 1962 innocent-passage regulation, the 17 Feb 1969 continental-shelf announcement) plus the cooperative 1969 shelf delimitation with Malaysia. No pre-1970 Indonesian statement on the Chinese claim, Natuna naval patrol or fishing-boat detention could be verified; Indonesia says it first became aware of the nine-dash-line claim in 1993.

Sub-region key: **Natuna** = Natuna islands / Natuna Sea; **Far South** = southern SCS generally (Indonesia–Malaysia shelf off Peninsular Malaysia and Sarawak); **other** = archipelago-wide / not SCS-specific.

## Unverified leads

- **Early Natuna oil concessions (1968–1971).** Secondary sources consistently say Agip discovered Natuna D-Alpha (East Natuna) in 1973 and Conoco discovered Udang (West Natuna, Block B) in 1974, implying production-sharing contracts in the Natuna Sea around 1968–71; an Agip seismic vintage of 1970 is recorded for the NE Natuna area. None of the sources opened gives the PSC signature dates. Check: Pertamina/IPA histories, SEC filings beyond the portions read, or Minerals Yearbook 1969–1971 (UW-Madison copy blocked to the page fetch). Sources seen: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Natuna_gas_field ("discovered in 1973 by Agip"); https://www.vangorselslist.com/hcc_occ.html ("D-Alpha-1973", "Udang-1974" (Conoco)); https://archives.datapages.com/data/southeast-asia-petroleum-exploration-society/data/022/022001/1_seapex0220046.htm ("vintage 1970 (AGIP)"); https://journals.csis.or.id/index.php/analisis/article/download/280/666/1375 (Conoco, Inca and Agip SCS blocks mapped as of 15 Aug 1976; Agip block overlapping South Vietnam's 1971 "Saigon decree" shelf claim).
- **Indonesian position on China's SCS claim before 1970.** Nothing found. Indonesia's foreign ministry states Indonesia first became aware of the nine-dash-line claim in 1993 (Ali Alatas asked Qian Qichen for clarification): https://www.thejakartapost.com/news/2020/01/16/now-and-then-china-s-incursions-natuna ("in 1993 — when Indonesia first became aware of the nine-dash-line claim"). Treat the pre-1970 position as "none documented".
- **1960s naval patrols / detention of Chinese or Taiwanese fishing boats around Natuna.** No documented incident found in any source opened; the earliest Natuna fishing-enforcement incidents in the literature are 1990s–2010s. A targeted check of Indonesian naval histories (e.g., TNI-AL Konfrontasi-era operations 1963–66 in the Natuna/Anambas area) would be needed.
- **1958 Riau Province establishment.** natunakab.go.id states "Riau menjadi sebuah Propinsi pada tanggal 5 Maret 1958 dengan ibukotanya Tanjungpinang"; the enabling statute (Emergency Law 19/1957 and Law 61/1958) was not opened, so the date is left unverified.
- **Text of the 17 Feb 1969 Announcement.** Only its date and content-as-summarised were verified (via Law 1/1973 recitals and secondary sources). The English text is probably in UN Legislative Series ST/LEG/SER.B/15 or US State Dept files; a CIA FOIA item titled "An Indonesian view of the offshore boundary question" (https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/node/1945512) could not be opened (the page fetch returned the Reading Room homepage).
- **Blocked:** Cambridge chapter "Natunas: territorial integrity in the forefront of Indonesia-China relations" (https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/natunas/...) — fetch permission not granted.
