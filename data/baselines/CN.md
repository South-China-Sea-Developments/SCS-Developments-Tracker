# China (People's Republic): baseline actions before 1970

Andrew Chubb's own zero-baseline list, reproduced verbatim from the methodology appendix to Chubb (2021), "PRC Assertiveness in the South China Sea: Measuring Continuity and Change, 1970–2015", *International Security* 45(3), Harvard Dataverse doi:10.7910/DVN/3Y7NRU (CC0). His framing: "The list below shows a selection of known instances of PRC actions over the South China Sea between 1949 and 1969 that provides the zero-baseline for comparing the PRC's assertiveness between 1970 and 2015." Short references (Zhang NSQDDSJ, Lei NSZGSZH, Fravel *Strong Borders*, Samuels *Contest*) are his; the full citations are in the appendix.

| Period | Action (Chubb's wording) |
|---|---|
| 1949, November | First PRC map published incorporating ROC eleven-dash line (“Xin Zhongghuo diyizhang ditu,”) |
| 1950, May 20 | PRC publicly asserts sovereignty over Spratlys in RMRB p.1 articles condemning Philippines’ claims (Chen, “China’s ASEAN policy,” p. 149). |
| 1950-1956 | Qionghai fishers operate in Spratlys (Zhang, NSQDDSJ, 3). But according to another source the PRC prohibited its citizens from fishing in Spratlys form 1954 onwards (Guo Jinfu, “Nansha yuye ziyuan,” p. 298). |
| 1950-1956 | Guangdong Hainan Administrative Region allegedly erects weather stations, conducts surveys, extracts phosophorous and “manages fishing” in Paracels. (Zhang, NSQDDSJ, p. 3). |
| 1951, August | Zhou Enlai publicly asserts sovereignty over four South China Sea “island groups” (Samuels, Contest, pp. 78-9). |
| 1956 | PRC occupies Amphritrite Group in north-eastern Paracels. |
| 1956-1959 | PRC mines more than 100,000 tons of phosphates in Paracels. By 1961 the supply was exhausted (Lei, NSZGSZH, p. 133). |
| 1958 | PRC Territorial Sea Declaration asserts territorial sovereignty and seas around the four South China Sea “island groups,” but not Diaoyu Islands. |
| 1959 | Office for Zhongsha, Xisha and Nansha Affairs established. |
| 1959 | first PLAN patrol of Paracels (Fravel, Strong Borders, p. 275n25). |
| 1960 | construction of radio masts & observation tower at Woody Island. (Fravel, Strong Borders, p. 275). |
| 1960 | PLAN conducts sixteen patrols to protect Chinese fishers in the Paracels. (Fravel, Strong Borders, p. 275). |
| 1960-1973 | Average of 5 PLAN patrols per year in the Paraces (Fravel, Strong Borders, p. 275). |
| 1958-1971 | China issues 497 protests and warnings over US surveillance above the Paracels (Fravel, Strong Borders, p. 275n32). |

## Scope

The PRC only. Actions of the Republic of China before 1949 and after its relocation to Taiwan are in `TW.md`; the 1947 eleven-dash line map belongs to the ROC list and appears here only as the map the PRC republished in 1949.
