# Vietnam: baseline actions before 1970

Scope note. This list covers the two Vietnamese states of 1945–1969: the State of Vietnam (SVN, Bảo Đại government, 1949–55) and its successor the Republic of Vietnam (RVN, Saigon, 1955–75), which held and administered the Paracels (Crescent Group) and claimed the Spratlys; and the Democratic Republic of Vietnam (DRV, Hanoi), which made no claim of its own in this period and whose recorded behaviour on the islands consisted of statements supportive of the PRC position. Entries are sovereignty statements and protests, laws and administrative acts, occupations and garrisons, patrols, surveys, weather-station operation, phosphate extraction, detentions of foreign fishermen and uses or threats of force; the DRV items and one Gulf of Tonkin transfer are included as a record of behaviour even though they are not assertive. The core sources are the RVN Ministry of Foreign Affairs White Paper (Saigon, 1975), the SRV MFA white papers of 1981 and 1988, PRC MFA statements of 1959, US FRUS documents of June 1956, and Stein Tønnesson's 2001–2002 papers; where only a Vietnamese government source supports an entry this is flagged. Dates follow the source; discrepancies are noted.

Actor codes: SVN = State of Vietnam (1949–55); RVN = Republic of Vietnam (from Oct 1955); DRV = Democratic Republic of Vietnam.

## Unverified leads

- **"1956 Paracel incident / detention of PRC fishermen".** No source opened records an RVN detention of PRC fishermen in 1956. The only 1956 Paracel episode found is the 10 June 1956 Robert Island alert (FRUS d186–187), in which the GVN reported a PRC landing that the US could not confirm. The PRC MFA's 27 Feb 1959 statement refers back only to PRC statements of 1951 and 1956, not to an earlier RVN seizure. If a 1956 detention exists it may be in Samuels, *Contest for the South China Sea* (1982) pp. 81–86, or Chemillier-Gendreau (2000), neither of which could be opened (Google Books API quota exhausted; no open copy found).
- **April 1959: RVN marines occupy four islets in the Paracels; 160 fishermen arrested; release 9 March 1959.** Only in a 1998 *China Post* column reposted by Taiwan's National Policy Foundation (https://www.npf.org.tw/3/6121): "Undaunted, South Vietnam sent marines to the Paracels to occupy four islets in April 1959." Its "160 fishermen" conflicts with the 82 given by both the PRC and Vietnamese governments; the 9 March release date is plausible (the PRC's 5 Apr 1959 statement says the fishermen had been released after the 27 Feb protest) but is unconfirmed.
- **27 Oct 1955 ICAO Manila meeting**: China Daily says "Democratic Republic of Vietnam" delegates did not object to a resolution asking Taiwan to improve weather observation in the Spratlys. PRC sources elsewhere attribute this to the South Vietnamese delegation; the DRV was not an ICAO member. Attribution unresolved; omitted from the table.
- **RVN protest to the ROC over the June 1956 occupation of Itu Aba** and **RVN note to the Philippines in 1956**: Tønnesson (EAI WP 71) confirms a protest "against the Cloma action" but no text, date or addressee was found.
- **Decree number for 13 Jul 1961**: 174-NV (RVN 1975 and SRV 1981/1988 white papers) vs 175-NV (Nguyen Ba Dien 2020). The white papers are followed here.
- **Trần Văn Hữu statement date**: 7 Sep 1951 (RVN 1975 white paper, citing the US Dept of State Record of Proceedings p. 292) vs "July 7, 1951" (SRV 1981 white paper, evidently a misprint; the conference sat 4–8 Sep 1951). The Record of Proceedings itself was not opened.
- **Nhân Dân coverage of the 1958 PRC declaration (6 and 22 Sep 1958) and DRV school atlases/textbooks** cited by Beijing: not opened; only the 1958 note and the 1956/1965 statements are confirmed by the SRV's own 1988 white paper.
- **SRV MFA white paper of 1979** ("Vietnam's Sovereignty over the Hoang Sa and Truong Sa Archipelagoes"): the CIA Reading Room copy (https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/node/1953519) redirected to the site home page and could not be opened.
